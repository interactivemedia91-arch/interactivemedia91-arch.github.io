TAG & TWINE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · Support

In short: Tag & Twine has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information about you. Your gift list stays on your iPhone.

Your list

The people, gifts, prices, budgets, hiding places and notes you enter are saved on your iPhone, where only Tag & Twine and its widgets can read them. The app never sends them anywhere. Like your other apps’ data, they are included in your iPhone’s own backups to iCloud or your computer, if you use them, under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Deleting the app deletes your list.

Backups you export

Settings › Export backup writes your whole list, hiding places included, to a file and opens the share sheet: you choose where it goes. The file isn’t locked, so anyone who has it can read your list. Import backup reads only the file you pick, and replaces your list with it after asking you.

App Lock

If you turn on App Lock, Tag & Twine asks iOS to check that it’s you with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone’s passcode. iOS does the check and tells the app only whether it worked: the app never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Widgets

The widgets read your list on your iPhone to show the days to go and how many gifts are left. They never show a name, a gift or a price.

Links

A link you add to a gift opens in your web browser when you tap it, and that website’s own privacy policy applies. Tag & Twine doesn’t load anything from the link itself.

Purchases

Tag & Twine is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing and tracking

Tag & Twine contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not share any information with anyone. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may pass us crash reports that don’t identify you.

Children

Tag & Twine does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
