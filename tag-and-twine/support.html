How do I add someone, and their gifts?

On the occasion screen, tap Add the first person; after that, the round + button adds more. Tap a person’s tag to open it, then tap Add gift or idea.

How do I mark a gift bought or wrapped?

Tap the stamp on the gift: IDEA turns to BOUGHT, then WRAPPED. To set it back, touch and hold the gift and choose another status, or open the gift and change Status.

How is the budget counted?

Each person can have a budget, and so can the occasion; left empty, the occasion’s budget adds up everyone’s. Spent is the price of every gift marked bought or wrapped, and the ribbon at the top fills as you spend. Ideas don’t count until you buy them.

Where’s my shopping list?

Tap the gifts bought count at the top of the occasion. It shows what’s still to buy, person by person, and who has nothing yet. Tap a stamp there to mark a gift bought.

Can I use it for birthdays and other occasions?

Yes. Tap the occasion’s name at the top, then New occasion. A new occasion can start from an earlier one, bringing its people and their budgets, and its unbought ideas if you like. Tap the name again to switch between occasions.

Will the widgets give the surprise away?

No. They show the days to go and how many gifts are left, never a name, a gift or a price. To add one, touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Tag & Twine. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap Customize.

How does App Lock work?

Turn it on in Settings (the gear at the top right). Tag & Twine then locks whenever you leave it and opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone’s passcode; the app switcher shows a cover instead of your list. App Lock needs a passcode on your iPhone. Anyone who knows that passcode can open the list too.

I’m getting a new iPhone. How do I keep my list?

If you set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or your computer), your list comes back with your other apps. To move it yourself, tap Export backup in Settings and save the file where the new iPhone can reach it, such as iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it. On the new iPhone, install Tag & Twine, tap Import backup in Settings and pick the file. Importing replaces the list on that iPhone, after asking. The file isn’t locked, so keep it somewhere private.

Can I share the list with my partner?

Not yet. Your list stays on this iPhone: there’s no syncing or sharing between phones. A backup file can be imported on another iPhone, but it replaces the list there, so it’s a copy, not a shared list.

Can I change the currency?

Yes, in Settings › Currency. Amounts are relabeled, not converted: $45 becomes €45.

Is there anything else to pay?

No. Tag & Twine is a one-time purchase: no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.
