Brumalis: Support & Privacy

Brumalis, snow that settles on your Mac

Support

Need help with Brumalis? Email interactivemedia91@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 days.

Where is Brumalis after I close the welcome window?

In the menu bar, at the top right of your screen: click the snowflake to open its panel. Brumalis has no Dock icon.

How do I stop the snow?

Click the snowflake and turn off Snowing. To close the app completely, click Quit at the bottom of the panel.

Why doesn't snow settle on my maximized window?

Snow settles on the top edge of a window. When a window reaches all the way up to the menu bar, that edge is hidden behind the menu bar, so its snow is too. It still collects at the bottom of the screen.

Will it slow down my Mac or drain my battery?

Brumalis is built to use very little power, and the panel shows how much it is using. It pauses on its own while your screen is locked, in Low Power Mode and behind full-screen apps.

Can I still click things through the snow?

Yes. The snow never takes a click: everything underneath works as usual.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short: Brumalis has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information about you.

Windows on your screen

So that snow can settle on your windows, Brumalis reads the position and size of the windows on your screen while it is snowing. It does not read their titles or what is inside them. This information stays on your Mac, is used only to draw the snow, and is not stored.

Your place and Apple Weather

If you choose a place, for real wind, daylight or the Outside mode, Brumalis sends that place's coordinates to Apple Weather to get its forecast, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The place and the latest forecast are stored on your Mac. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

Brumalis asks for your Mac's location only if you click Use my Mac's location. It then uses one approximate location to set your place, as described above. You can type a city instead, and you can turn location access off at any time in System Settings.

Purchases

Brumalis is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing and tracking

Brumalis contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not share any information with anyone.

Children

Brumalis does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
