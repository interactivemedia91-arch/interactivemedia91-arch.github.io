Hitchstead: Support & Privacy

Hitchstead, the hitch and rotation calendar for iPhone

Support

Need help with Hitchstead? Email interactivemedia91@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 days.

How do I share my rotation with my partner?

In Hitchstead, open Share and tap Publish to Apple Calendar. Then, in Apple's Calendar app, tap Calendars, the info button next to Hitchstead, and Add Person. Your partner doesn't need the app; changes you save in Hitchstead update that calendar.

Does my schedule move when I fly to another time zone?

No. Your rotation is kept in the time zone you set it up in, so your days stay on their dates wherever you are.

I already paid. How do I get the unlock back?

Open Settings in Hitchstead and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Why does Hitchstead ask for calendar access?

Only when you tap Publish, so it can create and update its own Hitchstead calendar. It never changes your other calendars or the events you add yourself.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: Hitchstead has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information about you.

Your schedule

Your rotation and the changes you save are stored on your iPhone, where the Hitchstead widget reads them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Calendar

If you publish, Hitchstead writes all-day events (such as "On hitch · Nights", "Travel" and "Home") into its own Hitchstead calendar on your device. If that calendar is in iCloud, Apple syncs it to your other devices under Apple's Privacy Policy, and anyone you invite to it can see those events. Removing Hitchstead doesn't delete that calendar; you can delete it in the Calendar app.

Exported files

An ICS file you export goes only where you choose to send it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Hitchstead only asks Apple, on your device, whether your Apple Account owns the unlock.

Sharing and tracking

Hitchstead contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not share any information with anyone.

Children

Hitchstead does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
