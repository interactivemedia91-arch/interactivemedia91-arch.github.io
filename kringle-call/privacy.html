Kringle Call: Support & Privacy

Kringle Call, a phone call with Santa

Support

Need help with Kringle Call? Email interactivemedia91@gmail.com. We usually reply within 1–2 days.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones with Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and newer, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Santa's listening and his answers run on the iPhone itself, which is why it needs one of these.

The app says Apple Intelligence is off or not ready

Turn it on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Right after you turn it on, the iPhone may need some time, on Wi-Fi and charging, to finish downloading it. Until then your child can still have a short pre-recorded call with Santa.

Santa can't hear my child

Check that the microphone is allowed in Settings › Kringle Call. During the call, your child speaks after the little chime; a quiet room helps. Tap the microphone button to talk again.

How many calls are free?

One short call with Santa every day, ending with a little kindness mission. The village, the countdown widget and Santa's tracker on Christmas Eve are free too. The Christmas Pass adds longer calls, Santa knowing each child, the Advent calendar, the reindeer, letters to Santa and more.

How do I restore the Christmas Pass on another iPhone?

Open the parents' area (the button at the top right), go to Settings, tap See what's included, then Restore Purchases. With Family Sharing turned on, the Pass works for everyone in your family.

Will Santa promise my child a present?

No. Santa never promises a gift and never says no to a wish. In the parents' area you can also list things Santa must never promise, and after each call you see what was wished for.

When can we follow Santa's sleigh?

On Christmas Eve, 24 December. The tracker shows Santa's flight around the world on a globe, and you can put a countdown to his visit on your Lock Screen.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: Kringle Call was built so that nothing a child says ever leaves the iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information about you or your child.

How Santa hears and answers

  • Listening: the microphone is used only during a call with Santa, when naming the reindeer and when dictating a letter. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on the iPhone itself; the audio is not recorded or kept.
  • Answering: Santa's replies are written by Apple Intelligence's on-device language model, on the iPhone itself. Santa's voice is generated on the iPhone as well.
  • Downloads: the first time, iOS may download Apple's English speech model. That download comes from Apple and contains nothing about your family.

What stays on the iPhone

What a parent enters (children's first names, ages, things Santa may praise, wishes, good deeds, scheduled calls) and what calls produce (short summaries for the parent, the wish list, kindness missions, letters to Santa and his replies, opened Advent doors) are stored only in Kringle Call's own storage on the iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The parents' area is protected with Face ID or the device passcode.

Notifications

"Santa calls you" uses local notifications scheduled on the iPhone. No push service is involved.

Purchases

The Christmas Pass is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The purchase is confirmed by Apple's StoreKit on the iPhone.

Sharing and tracking

Kringle Call contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not share any information with anyone.

Children

Kringle Call is meant to be used by children with a parent nearby. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children (including under COPPA). Purchases and the parents' area sit behind a parental check.
